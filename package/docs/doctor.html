The arithmetic: with n runs per side and a tie-splitting win rule, an inert candidate wins with q = (1 - P(tie))/2; the round-level rate is 1 - (1-q)k. P(tie) is the central term of the difference of two Binomial(n, p) draws; q → 1/2 as n grows. Measured on a production agent (replay flip 11.7%) the same arithmetic held to three decimals - the paper in the repo has the receipts.
